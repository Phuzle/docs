## What is Messages?

**Messages** is an Android SMS app, published by Phuzle Labs, that replaces your phone's default
texting app. It automatically sorts incoming texts into Personal, OTP, Transactions, and
Promotions, and surfaces one-time verification codes for one-tap copying — all using rules that
run entirely on your device. This page is the official homepage for the Messages app.

## Why Messages asks for the permissions it does

- **Default SMS app role, SMS/contacts access** — required by Android for any app to send,
  receive, and organize text messages at all; this is not optional for an SMS client.
- **Google Sign-In and Google Drive access** — only requested if you turn on Drive backup
  yourself. Messages requests the narrow `drive.appdata` scope, which limits it to a private,
  per-app folder in your Drive that isn't visible in your regular files and that no one at Phuzle
  Labs can read. It's used solely to store and restore your own encrypted message backup. See the
  [Privacy Policy](/messages/privacy) for the full breakdown of every permission and data type.

## Key features

- **Automatic categorization** — Personal, OTP, Transactions, and Promotions, sorted by rules
  that run entirely on-device.
- **Instant OTP copy** — one-tap copy for verification codes, plus a quick-swap notification when
  a new code arrives.
- **Passbook** — a running ledger of card/bank transactions, extracted automatically from your
  transaction texts.
- **Local-first** — your messages are never uploaded anywhere. Optional encrypted backup to your
  own Google Drive is available, off by default.

## Get the app (Closed Testing)

Messages is currently in a closed testing phase on the Google Play Store. To try it out before general release, follow these steps:

1. **Join the waitlist:** First, join the [Testers - Messages - Phuzle Labs](https://groups.google.com/g/testers-messages-phuzle-labs) Google Group. Access to the app is restricted to members of this group.
2. **Opt-in to testing:** Once your join request is approved or you become a member, visit the [testing opt-in page](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.phuzle.labs.messages) and click "Become a tester".
3. **Download the app:** After opting in, you can [download Messages from the Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.phuzle.labs.messages).

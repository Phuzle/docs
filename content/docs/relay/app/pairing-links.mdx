---
title: Pairing links
description: How QR codes and links add a phone to a topic.
---

In the console, open a topic and choose **Pair a phone**. You get a QR code and a link that looks
like `https://relay.phuzle.com/p#<token>`.

![The pairing dialog in the console](/relay/console-pair.png)

On the phone, tap **Pair** in the Relay app and choose **Scan QR code** or **Enter link or code**.
With the app installed the link also opens it directly (Android App Links), and the app accepts
`relay://pair?t=<token>`. Without the app, the page explains how to install it.

<Screens>

![Choosing how to pair](/relay/android-pair.png)

![Confirming the topic](/relay/android-confirm.png)

![Paired](/relay/android-paired.png)

</Screens>

- The token is in the URL **fragment**, so it is never sent to a web server or logged by one.
- It is **single-use** and lasts 15 minutes, 1 hour or 24 hours, your choice.
- The app first *previews* the topic and organization without using the token, then you confirm.
- The console dialog closes and shows "Phone paired" the moment the phone confirms.
- Cancel an unused link from the same dialog.
- Whoever confirms first gets the device paired; treat a link like a password until it is used.

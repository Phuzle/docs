---
title: Plans and limits
description: What each plan includes and how limits behave.
---

| | Free | Pro | Team |
|---|---|---|---|
| Topics | 10 | 50 | 200 |
| Devices per topic | 5 | 25 | 100 |
| API keys | 10 | 50 | 200 |
| Triggers per day (org) | 1,000 | 50,000 | 500,000 |
| History and activity | 24 hours | 90 days | 1 year |
| Members | 1 | 1 | 10 |
| Plaintext size | 2 KiB | 2 KiB | 2 KiB |
| Actions per message | 3 | 3 | 3 |

<Callout type="warn" title="Paid plans are not on sale yet">
	Pro and Team are being rolled out by invitation. Numbers may change before general availability.
</Callout>

- Plans belong to the **organization**, not a person.
- Going over the daily cap returns `429 quota_exceeded` with a `Retry-After` header.
- **Downgrading never deletes anything.** Existing topics, devices and keys keep working; you just can't create new ones above the lower limit. History older than the new window is removed.
- Payload size and actions per message are the same on every plan.

![The Relay console overview](/relay/console-overview.png)

| Page | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Messages, deliveries, failures and latency for the period you pick, plus where messages come from and your busiest topics. Updates live. |
| **Send** | Compose and send a full notification to a topic without any code. See below. |
| **Activity** | The latest messages across every topic, with how many phones received each. Message content is never stored. |
| **Topics** | Create topics and open one to see its phones, activity, integrations and settings. |
| **Devices** | Every paired phone and when it was last seen. Give a phone a nickname to tell it apart. |
| **API keys** | Create, rotate and revoke the keys your code uses to send. |
| **Members** | Invite teammates with a link and set their role. See [Members and roles](/relay/concepts/members-and-roles). |
| **Usage** | How much of your plan's limits you are using. See [Plans and limits](/relay/concepts/limits). |

The account menu (top right) has your profile, billing, organization settings and the theme.
Press `⌘K` to jump to any page or topic.

## Send a message from the console

**Send** lets you write and send a complete notification to every phone on a topic, with no SDK, CLI or API call.
Choose the topic, then add:

- **Title and message**, and a **priority** (Minimal to Urgent).
- **Icon** (one of about 40 built-in ones), **link** (https), **tags** (up to 5), a **group** that stacks related notifications, how long to **keep trying**
  if a phone is offline, and a **replace key** so a newer message replaces an undelivered older one.
- Up to three **action buttons**. Each either **opens a link** in the phone's browser (a dashboard, a runbook) or
  **sends a request** to your HTTPS endpoint from the phone; mark a request **destructive** and the phone asks for
  confirmation first. Headers and a body are optional.

A live preview shows how it will look and how much of the 2 KiB encrypted message you have used. The message is
encrypted in your browser with the same code as the SDK, so Relay only ever sees ciphertext. Sends from the
console count towards your daily limit and appear in Activity and Overview as "Console messages".

## Rename a phone

On **Devices** (or a topic's phone list), use the pencil to give a phone a **nickname** such as "Office phone".
The name and model the phone reported stay visible underneath. You can also rename the phone in the app, under
**Settings → Security → This phone**; the last change wins. The nickname is shown to every organization the phone
is paired with.

## Live updates

You don't need to refresh. When a phone pairs, a message is sent or a delivery finishes, the pages
that show it update on their own.

## Destructive actions ask first

Removing a phone, revoking or rotating a key, removing a member and deleting an organization or a
topic all open a confirmation. The heaviest ones ask you to type the name to confirm.

## Roles

Owners and admins manage everything. Members can see topics, activity and usage but can't change
them.

## Support activity

**Support activity** (owners and admins) lists everything Relay support did on your organization and
every time we looked, in plain sentences, newest first: a plan change, a limit raised for you, an API
key revoked, a source paused, a suspension, or a read-only view of your console.

Use **Ask support** on the same page to send us a question (a subject and what is happening; please
don't paste message contents, keys or tokens). Our answer appears under **Your questions**. You can have
three open questions at a time.

Relay support can open a **read-only view** of your console to help you. It lasts 30 minutes, shows only
your organization, can't change anything and can't see your profile or other organizations. A banner
tells everyone looking at the console that support is viewing, with a **Leave support view** button, and
the view is listed in Support activity with the reason. Support never sees message content: it is
encrypted before it reaches Relay.

## Notices and suspension

If Relay shows a notice above the console (a maintenance window, an incident), it is for everyone. If an
organization is **suspended**, the console says so: it can still be read, but no messages are accepted
and nothing can be changed until support restores it.

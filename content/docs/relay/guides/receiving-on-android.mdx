---
title: Receiving on Android
description: Permissions, battery settings, mute and backups.
---

- **Notifications permission** (Android 13+) is required to show alerts.
- **Battery optimisation:** if notifications arrive late, exempt Relay from battery optimisation.
  The app shows a notice when this applies. Some manufacturers need an extra "autostart" setting.
- **Priority channels:** `min`, `low`, `default`, `high` and `urgent` map to separate notification
  channels you can tune in system settings.
- **Mute a topic** to stop sounds while still storing messages in the inbox.
- **Unsubscribe** from a topic in the app; the console also lets you remove a device.
- **Themes:** system, light, dark, or AMOLED black.
- **Key storage:** by default your keys are in your Google backup, so a new phone keeps its identity.
  In Settings → Backup & key storage you can switch to **Hardware-bound**: keys are sealed by the
  phone's secure hardware and never leave it, so a new phone has to pair again. Switching keeps the
  same identity; nothing needs re-pairing.
- Reinstalling the app without a backup creates a new device identity, so you need to pair again.

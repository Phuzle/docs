## The app

- **Inbox.** Messages grouped by day, with a count of what needs you, what arrived today and what is
  unread. Filter to **Needs you** (high or urgent and not yet acknowledged) or **Urgent**. Tap a
  message to see it, press its buttons, and check when it was delivered.
- **Topics.** Everything you're subscribed to. Tap one to mute it or unsubscribe. **Pair** adds
  another: scan a QR code or enter a link.
- **Settings.** Theme, notification and battery settings, and your keys.

## Notifications

- **Notifications permission** (Android 13+) is required to show alerts. The app asks right after
  you pair.
- **Battery optimisation:** if notifications arrive late, exempt Relay from battery optimisation.
  The app shows a notice when this applies. Some manufacturers need an extra "autostart" setting.
- **Priority channels:** `min`, `low`, `default`, `high` and `urgent` map to separate notification
  channels you can tune in system settings (**Settings → Sound and importance**).
- **Per-topic settings:** open a topic from the Topics tab to tune it. Choose the lowest priority that should notify (the rest
  still land in the inbox), mute for an hour, eight hours or until morning, turn sound and vibration off,
  hide the message on the lock screen (show all, title only or hide), and set daily quiet hours, with
  urgent messages optionally breaking through.
- **Unsubscribe** from a topic in the app; the console also lets you remove a device.

## Name this phone

**Settings → Security → This phone → Rename** gives the phone a nickname. It appears in the console next to the
model the phone reports, and you can change it there too. Renaming needs a connection to Relay.

## Archive and delete

Open a message and use the icons at the top right. **Archive** moves it out of the inbox into **Archived**
(restore it any time). **Delete** moves it to **Trash**, where it stays for 30 days before it is removed for
good; you can restore it from there, or choose **Delete forever** / **Empty Trash**. Both show an **Undo**.
These actions only affect the copy on your phone.

## About

**Settings → About** links to the website, these docs, support, the privacy policy and the terms.

## Themes

System, light, dark or AMOLED black. Settings → Appearance.

## Keys and backups

By default your keys are in your Google backup, so a new phone keeps its identity. In
**Settings → Key storage** you can switch to **Hardware-bound**: keys are sealed by the phone's
secure hardware and never leave it, so a new phone has to pair again. Switching keeps the same
identity; nothing needs re-pairing. Reinstalling the app without a backup creates a new device
identity, so you need to pair again.

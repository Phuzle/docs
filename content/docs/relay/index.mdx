## What is Relay?

**Relay** sends notifications from your code, CI or servers to your phone, without you building a
mobile app and without handing your message content to anyone in between.

- **End-to-end encrypted.** Your SDK or CLI encrypts each message on your machine for each paired
  phone. Relay and Google's FCM only ever carry ciphertext.
- **Actionable.** Attach up to three buttons ("Acknowledge", "Restart api-3") that fire an HTTPS
  request straight from the notification.
- **Pair with a link or QR.** Open the link on a phone with the Relay app, confirm, done.
- **Send from anywhere.** The [SDK](/relay/guides/sdk), the [CLI](/relay/guides/cli) or
  the [Trigger API](/relay/reference/api).
- **GitHub, built in.** A webhook turns repository events into notifications.
- **A live console.** See deliveries, failures and latency as they happen, and invite your team.

## How it fits together

1. You create a **topic** (for example `acme-alerts`) in the console.
2. You **pair** one or more phones to it with a single-use link or QR code.
3. Your code **sends** a message with an API key. The SDK or CLI fetches the topic's phone keys,
   encrypts one envelope per phone, and posts them to Relay.
4. Relay queues the envelopes, delivers them through FCM, and each phone decrypts and shows the
   notification.

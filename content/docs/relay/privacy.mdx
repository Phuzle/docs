**Effective date:** October 5, 2026 (draft — pending legal review)

This policy covers the Relay Android app and the Relay service at relay.phuzle.com, published by Phuzle Labs.

## The short version

Notifications you send directly through the SDK or CLI are end-to-end encrypted. Relay and Google
cannot read their content. We store account and routing metadata, never message content.

## What we collect

- **Account:** the email, name and Google account identifier from Google sign-in, and your org name.
- **Devices:** a public encryption key, a public signing key, your Firebase Cloud Messaging token, device model, OS and app version, and last-seen time.
- **Routing metadata:** topics, subscriptions, message ids, size, priority, timestamps, and delivery status (queued, sent, delivered, failed). Delivery logs are kept for 30 days on the free tier.
- **Secrets:** API keys, pairing tokens and sessions are stored only as one-way hashes. Webhook secrets are stored encrypted.

## What we do not collect

Titles, bodies, actions and URLs of directly sent notifications. Ciphertext exists on our servers only while queued and is removed when delivery completes.

## Server-assisted sources

For integrations such as GitHub, Relay formats and encrypts the event itself, so we process that event content in memory without storing it. These are labelled "server-assisted" in the app.

## Third parties

Google Firebase Cloud Messaging carries ciphertext to your device. Google handles sign-in. We do not sell data or use it for advertising.

## Your choices

Remove a device or topic in the console or app, revoke keys, or delete your account by contacting us. Private keys never leave your phone; uninstalling the app destroys them.
